======================================================================
INTERPRETATION LIMITATION
======================================================================

These results compare PolarGuard-generated feasible routes with recorded historical AIS trajectories between matched start and destination locations.
A shorter modeled route should not be interpreted as proof that the historical vessel route was incorrect or inefficient.
The real Polarstern trajectory may include scientific operations, station keeping, maneuvering requirements, weather information, operational decisions, and other constraints not represented by this prototype.

The validation demonstrates end-to-end historical route generation and comparison under PolarGuard's modeled sea-ice, uncertainty, iceberg, and vessel constraint framework.
